# [[IKN 2026]] revised discussion draft

The IKN 2026 concept is a discussion draft before announcement: it runs 6 November 12.00–8 November 11.00 at the supplied location, uses three participation prices (AC lodging Rp265,000 for 6 slots; non-AC lodging Rp200,000 for 4 slots; non-lodging Rp150,000 for 10 slots), has only 10 proper sleeping slots, and has a registration deadline of 16 October 24.00.

The draft keeps the event largely self-managed and minimally scheduled. It includes trivia, awards, and the annual meeting; excludes catering; requires registration through a Google Form, payment to BCA Niko, and payment-proof upload; and specifies the supplied participant and venue rules.
