Id like to say we make all kinds of custom. but really we are kinda limited to aluminium, stainless steel, GRC and fiber glass.

alumunium cast is the one we can do best. we understand the proper chain and have decent team to do all of it. Theres not really a limitation on how big the cast aluminium sclupture can be. i think its mostly project budget and time is the limit. Alumunium casts sclupture tend to have a 'middle ground' pricing when compared to the other materials that we do. i suppose the problem is that to make alumunium sclupture we need to make the mold first, for a one off project that could be out of budget. so we have altenative of cheaper 3d printed mold. they are cheap but need a lot more time to produce the mold. at minimum twice the current time for fiberglass mold. its pure alumunium so outdoor use is of absolutely no problem, they can be painted but the nature of pure alumunium means that the paint can peel off in like 5 years, this is due to the alumunium oxide growing beneath the paint and peels off the paint.

While we dont stock much fiberglass sclupture we still can produce them. why dont we stock it? its because previous owner artist my dad does not like how the material feels. Still we extensively uses fiberglass to produce the mold for our aluminium scluptures. so its kinda like a byproduct of the alumunium cast production chain. we can simply paint that mold to get a finished product. I dont usually offer fiberglass slupture still. Again in this material options theres a similar feeling material in 3d prints, with the advantage of cheaper production  (no need to make fiberglass mold) but need a lot more time. size wise similar to alumunium sclupture im not sure if theres a limit on size. its the byproduct of the casting prodcution chain anyway.

GRC is much the same, using the same fiberglass mold but filling it with grc we can produce GRC statues in the same mold for the fiberglass mold. also no limit on the size we can give the inner part of the statues a steel reinforcement. but i will be VERY heavy compared to alumunium and fiberglass. heres i havent found anyway to cheapen the prodcution via 3d printing. the cost is going to be like standard fiberglass sclupture.

now we move on to the stainless steel material. when i say stainless steel material really what im going is like pieces of stainless steel. those fiberglass molds really came handy that we can use in many many production method. using those fiberglass molds we can stich together pieces of stainless pipe. since the mold is unlimited in size this method follows the same. pricing would be a lot more expensive since we need to weld each and every piece.

however remember that stainless piece? we can exchange that to other metals as well. with alumunium being the most popular one of them all. can be custom casted small alumunium pieces or aluminium pipe and wires. these will have cheaper price than the stainless pipe but more than the casted sclupture?

But really how do we decide with material to use? and how much is it to price it?

as a rule of thumb i try to recomend alumunium cast as much as possible. its the one we master the best. have a decent price to performance ratio for outdoor sclupture. If customer want a unique look or looking for that sweet stainless chrome we can obviously do the stainless sclupture as well. 

fiberglass and GRC sclupture would only be recomended if budget is really an issue. which it often is.

pricing it would be based on first how hard is it to design? how much material would this design take for a particular material? how long do we have to create it? standard time is 12 weeks but we can shorten that for a rush order. however like all art it came down to is it a good piece? 

personally i would rather leave a custom work alone rather than not delivering a good product.
