# Scene 6A — Prison Route

## What the players see

The miner’s partner is tied to a stone post.

He is:

- bruised
- scared
- dehydrated
- marked with blood
- meant for sacrifice

There may be one lizardman guard.

## Pressure clock

If they came here first:

**Clock 2: Drums begin.**

If they delayed:

**Clock 3: Prisoner dragged to pool.**

## Expected feeling

**Rescue under threat.**

They should feel:

> “We can save him, but every second spent here gives the brute more time with the map.”

## What the prisoner says

Keep it short.

```
“The big one has the map.”“They’re taking everything to the pool.”“They feed people to the crocodile.”“If blood hits the water, it comes.”
```

## Complication

Freeing him costs something.

Pick one:

- The knots are wet and tight.
- The lock is bone-and-rust.
- He is too weak to move quickly.
- A guard runs to warn the brute.
- The drums get louder.

## Pressure result

After rescue or delay, advance to:

**Clock 3: Prisoner dragged to pool**

unless they saved him fast.

## End the scene when

They decide whether to escape with him or push toward the map.

# Scene 6B — Loot Route

## What the players see

Stolen cargo from the ambush.

Useful items:

- rope
- oil
- dried meat
- boat hooks
- miner tools
- one healing item
- broken shield
- spare torches
- lizardmen weapons
- maybe their missing gear

## Pressure clock

Entering this room advances the clock.

**Clock 3: Prisoner dragged to pool.**

## Expected feeling

**Temptation with cost.**

They should feel:

> “This room helps us, but we are losing time.”

## GM purpose

Reward exploration without making it free.

## Useful finds

Let them grab 1–2 useful things quickly.

If they search carefully, give more, but advance pressure again.

## Pressure spike

While they loot:

> “From the center tunnel, chains scrape across stone. The prisoner is being moved.”

## End the scene when

They leave with supplies and understand the sacrifice is advancing.

---

# Scene 6C — Croc Pool Route

## What the players see

A large flooded chamber.

Details:

- Black water fills half the room.
- Bone piles line the edge.
- Lizardmen chant.
- The lizard brute stands near the pool.
- The sealed map tube hangs from his belt, neck, or shrine hook.
- The prisoner is either already here or about to be dragged in.

## Pressure clock

At minimum:

**Clock 3: Prisoner dragged to pool.**

If the party used fire/smoke or caused major noise:

**Clock 4: Blood hits water.**

## Expected feeling

**Boss confrontation and ritual danger.**

They should feel:

> “The map is here. The prisoner is here or coming here. The monster is below us.”

## GM purpose

Put all major threats in one place.

This is the climax chamber.

## The brute’s role

The brute is not just “the boss.”

He is the Croc God’s champion.

He wants:

```
Protect the map.Complete the feeding.Prove strength.Wake the god only when useful.
```

## Brute lines

Use short lines.

```
“Map belongs to deep water.”“Meat was promised.”“You bring more meat?”“God eats first.”
```

## End the scene when

Combat, negotiation, theft, or ritual disruption begins.

---

# Scene 7 — Brute Conflict

## What happens

The party faces the brute and lizardmen around the pool.

Do not run this as a static fight.

Run it as a fight around a ritual clock.

## Pressure clock

During the conflict:

```
Round 1: Prisoner near pool.Round 2: Brute cuts blood into water.Round 3: Water moves.Round 4: Croc wakes.
```

## Expected feeling

**Escalating danger.**

They should feel:

> “Winning the fight is not enough. We need the map and the prisoner before the room breaks.”

## GM purpose

Force movement.

Good goals during the fight:

- Cut prisoner loose.
- Grab map tube.
- Push lizardmen into water.
- Distract brute.
- Use meat to bait the croc.
- Collapse a ledge.
- Run.

## Important rule

The brute can be defeated.

The Croc God should not feel defeatable tonight.

## End the scene when

Blood hits the water or the map/prisoner situation is resolved.

---

# Scene 8 — Croc Wakes

## What happens

The water becomes still.

Then the giant croc rises.

First, kill or maim a lizardman to show scale.

> “The water erupts. A lizardman disappears screaming. The scream stops under the surface.”

## Pressure clock

**Clock 5: Croc wakes.**

## Expected feeling

**Panic and scale shift.**

They should feel:

> “This is no longer a fair fight. This is survival.”

## GM purpose

Change the objective.

Before:

```
Beat the brute.Get the map.Save the prisoner.
```

Now:

```
Get out alive with whatever you managed to secure.
```

## Croc actions

Use one per round.

|Croc Action|Result|
|---|---|
|Bite|Removes a lizardman or threatens a player|
|Tail smash|Breaks stone, blocks a route|
|Surge|Water floods the chamber|
|Death roll|Drags someone or something under|
|Roar|Causes panic and failed morale|
|Body slam|Opens a new escape crack|

## End the scene when

The party chooses escape over continued fighting.

---

# Scene 9 — Escape Scene

## What happens

The cave becomes unstable.

Pick 2–3 complications.

```
Smoke fills the tunnel.Water rises.The prisoner cannot run.The brute blocks the exit.The map tube falls near the pool.Lizardmen flee in every direction.The lookout ledge cracks.The front entrance is crowded with panicked enemies.
```

## Pressure clock

**Clock 6: Cave becomes escape scene.**

## Expected feeling

**Desperate exit.**

They should feel:

> “We are not cleaning this cave. We are surviving it.”

## GM purpose

End with momentum.

Go around the table.

Each player says how they help the escape.

Examples:

- Carry the prisoner.
- Hold off lizardmen.
- Throw meat to distract the croc.
- Use rope on the lookout ledge.
- Grab the map from mud.
- Break boats loose.
- Lead the party through smoke.

## Failure costs

Do not stop the escape on one failed roll.

Use costs:

- lose gear
- take damage
- map gets wet or torn
- prisoner worsens
- brute escapes
- croc follows to cave mouth
- someone exits separated from the group

## End the scene when

They burst out of the cave.

---

# Scene 10 — Session Ending

## Best ending

The party escapes with the map and prisoner.

## Expected feeling

**Hard-earned victory plus new mystery.**

Use this final beat:

> “The rescued man coughs river water from his lungs, grabs the sealed tube, and goes pale. ‘This is not just a route to the mine. Someone marked something under it.’”

End there.
