Audit this Wiki topic:

$ARGUMENTS

This command is READ-ONLY.

Do not:

- edit files;
- create branches;
- commit;
- push;
- open a Pull Request.

Follow AGENTS.md.

Find the relevant Wiki page and its atomic source notes.

Check for:

1. important claims with weak or missing source support;
2. source notes containing information absent from the Wiki;
3. contradictions between source notes;
4. statements that may represent old plans as current facts;
5. possibilities presented as confirmed;
6. important knowledge gaps clearly exposed by existing evidence;
7. unnecessary duplication or confusing organization.

Report findings in priority order.

For each finding, cite the relevant Obsidian note titles.

Do not invent hypothetical gaps merely to make the article more complete.

Finish with one of:

- HEALTHY — no material problem found
- UPDATE NEEDED — existing evidence supports an update
- CLARIFICATION NEEDED — human input is required

Do not make the change.
