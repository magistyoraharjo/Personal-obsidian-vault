# Magis Work Model

Magis owns PT Timboel and is broadly involved across the company without a formal cross-functional role. A July 2026 note described his day-to-day role as art director. Across the notes, his attention spans sculpture, craft production, international commerce, custom projects, pricing, production systems, ERPNext, and financial reporting. [[Magis owns PT Timboel and works across functions]] [[Monday, 20-7-2026 11꞉58꞉23]]

The clearest working model is **owner-integrator**: Magis connects what the market may buy, what should be designed, how it can be made, what it should cost, and how the company should reliably deliver it. This is an interpretation across the source notes rather than a formal job title.

## Work domains

### Art direction and product development

Magis is a Kasongan craftsperson with an industrial-engineering education. His creative work emphasizes material exploration, especially animal and human forms, while considering international market demand. [[Monday, 20-7-2026 11꞉58꞉23]]

PT Timboel's products combine craft and industrial production across wood, cast aluminium, welded assemblies, stainless steel, GRC, terracotta, finishing, and packing. Some stages happen internally and others through specialist suppliers. [[Creating Items Bill of material]]

### Wholesale and export

The preferred customer is an established art-products importer or wholesaler who understands its own market, buys at container scale, can order repeatedly, and may develop new products with Magis. The commercial goal is therefore not merely selling individual sculptures. It is building durable product-market relationships with buyers who can distribute Timboel's work profitably. [[Ideal Customer]]

### Custom sculpture and public projects

Custom work begins with an external need and may pass through site research, sketches, stakeholder review, changed scope, maquettes, engineering, pricing, and deadline negotiation. The Sangiran proposal shows this process clearly: an educational six-statue concept changed into one large monumental figure after government review, forcing new design, structural, cost, and schedule decisions. [[hasil sangiran patung manusia purba]]

Tilatso Studio is a possible separate brand for non-mass custom work, especially sculpture prototypes and installations for younger artists. Its proposed promise is to preserve design intent while supplying confidentiality, reliable completion, and international quality. It is a documented proposal, not a confirmed operating business. [[Tilatso Profile]]

### Company systems

The notes also describe the intended operating machinery behind the art. ERPNext is meant to connect projects, tasks, material requests, purchasing, warehouses, stock movements, manufacturing, bills of materials, sales, and accounting. [[Timboel ERPNext Workflow]] [[PT Timboel ERPNext Operations]]

This system work is not separate from product work. Detailed BOM design translates a sculpture into stockable intermediate items, supplier processes, internal finishing, packaging, and cost rules. Inputs below 3% of HPP and labour may be consolidated into operational cost. [[Creating Items Bill of material]]

## Operating loop

Magis's work can be represented as one recurring loop:

1. **Find or recognize demand.** Start from an importer, custom client, artist, institution, exhibition, or commercial partnership. Prefer opportunities with repeatability, market knowledge, or strategic exposure. [[Ideal Customer]] [[Kerjasama display dengan yia]]
2. **Translate demand into an offer.** Define product, design intent, quantity, material, production time, deposit, exclusivity, certification, storage, packing, shipping, warranty, and support. Price is treated as a configurable bundle of value and risk rather than one fixed number. [[Wednesday, 25-3-2026 16꞉26꞉36 feature barang timboel]]
3. **Make the idea testable.** Use research, sketches, reference work, maquettes, prototypes, simulations, or a virtual model before committing to full production. [[hasil sangiran patung manusia purba]] [[how big things get done]]
4. **Engineer production.** Break the object into materials, intermediate goods, internal operations, outsourced operations, finishing, packing, and stock movements. [[Creating Items Bill of material]]
5. **Coordinate execution.** Represent custom orders, product development, and business development as projects; use tasks for progress and Material Requests for purchasing needs. [[Timboel ERPNext Workflow]]
6. **Build delivery and financial controls.** The notes call for photographic internal QC, a clearer post-Sales Order workflow, weekly receivables and payables review, and monthly balance-sheet and profit-and-loss reporting. These controls remain partly intended or unreliable rather than fully established. [[Friday, 03-4-2026 15꞉31꞉13 pembenaran system erpnext wiki]] [[Belajar Accounting]]
7. **Capture learning.** Reuse project data for reference-class estimates and modularize repeatable work. [[how big things get done]]

## How Magis works

### Systems before repetition

Recurring judgment tends to become a code, field, template, threshold, checklist, workflow, or automation. The goal is to avoid solving the same operational problem from zero. This appears in item-code conventions, multi-level BOMs, stock-entry rules, price levers, and accounting cadence. [[Creating Items Bill of material]] [[Timboel ERPNext Workflow]] [[Wednesday, 25-3-2026 16꞉26꞉36 feature barang timboel]] [[Belajar Accounting]]

### Simulation before commitment

A note records these project principles: plan vigorously, simulate early and repeatedly, build a one-to-one virtual model when possible, modularize, and estimate from a reference class before adjusting for differences. [[how big things get done]]

### Market-aware creativity

Creative and commercial thinking are intertwined. Material exploration and artistic form matter, but Magis also asks what sells internationally, what margin a buyer can earn, and what feature trade-offs make an offer workable. [[Monday, 20-7-2026 11꞉58꞉23]] [[Ideal Customer]] [[Wednesday, 25-3-2026 16꞉26꞉36 feature barang timboel]]

### Raw capture, later structure

Magis prefers capturing unstructured notes quickly and using AI to recommend metadata, links, project updates, and a morning digest. This separates thinking and observation from later organization. [[Obsidian workflow vision]]

## Delegation model

The documented pricing flow gives initial and parallel calculation work to named staff before sharing the result with marketing. Purchasing buys materials requested by projects. This supports a limited picture of delegated execution, but the wider reporting structure is not documented. [[Flow Querry harga]] [[Timboel ERPNext Workflow]]

This creates leverage, but also makes Magis a common integration point. When responsibilities, approval boundaries, or handoffs are vague, unresolved decisions tend to return to him. This is a working interpretation, not a documented organization chart.

## Strengths

- **Cross-domain translation:** connects artistic intent to materials, engineering, price, production, and market value.
- **Structural thinking:** decomposes complex objects and projects into reusable components and rules.
- **Commercial flexibility:** negotiates features and terms instead of relying only on discounts.
- **Long-horizon learning:** wants project history to improve later estimates and decisions.
- **Comfort with complexity:** works across physical craft, international sales, software systems, accounting, and institutional partnerships.

These strengths are inferred from repeated patterns across the operating, commercial, and project notes.

## Recurring constraints

- **Owner bottleneck:** broad involvement can make Magis the default point for decisions that lack a clear owner.
- **Unfinished handoffs:** the sequence from Sales Order through materials, production authorization, stock movement, and delivery is not fully defined. [[Friday, 03-4-2026 15꞉31꞉13 pembenaran system erpnext wiki]]
- **Control-system gaps:** receivables monitoring is described as unreliable enough to require manual checking. [[Belajar Accounting]]
- **Scope and deadline conflict:** stakeholder changes can sharply alter design, engineering, cost, and production time after work begins. [[hasil sangiran patung manusia purba]]
- **System-design overhead:** building robust rules improves repeatability, but unresolved rules can themselves block execution.
- **Portfolio spread:** product development, custom projects, export sales, operational repair, and possible new partnerships compete for the same owner attention.

## Current strategic shape

The evidence suggests four levels of work:

1. **Core engine:** make and sell Timboel art products, especially for international wholesale.
2. **Operational foundation:** improve ERPNext, costing, inventory, project control, and accounting reliability.
3. **Higher-variance projects:** handle custom sculpture, installation, and public-sector work with changing design, cost, and schedule constraints.
4. **Options:** test adjacent channels and structures such as Tilatso Studio, YIA display sales, and government cooperation. Their current status is not confirmed. [[Tilatso Profile]] [[Kerjasama display dengan yia]] [[meeting KSO dengan pak totok dan mas syaid]]

## Boundaries of this model

This model describes strong patterns in the notes, not a formal organization chart or complete job description. Ownership is current; the art-director description comes from July 2026. The owner-integrator framing, strengths, and bottlenecks are synthesis. Project notes often capture possibilities or unresolved work, so they do not prove that every initiative is active.
