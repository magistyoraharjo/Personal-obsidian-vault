# Magis and Jessy Wedding

Magis and Jessy's wedding is a multi-event celebration running from 26 September through 5 October 2026. The sequence includes family ceremonies in Sukoharjo and Candi Tirto, two community Pesta Rakyat events, a Solo reception, and a Candhari neighborhood reception. ## Guests And Purpose

The family ceremonies are planned at 100 guests for the lamaran, 50 for the siraman, and 300 for the akad. [[Magis and Jessy wedding guests confirmed]]

Resepsi Relasi is intended for Magis's mother's friends, business acquaintances, and other important people in Yogyakarta who could provide long-term value to Magis. It is expected to have around 300 guests. [[Magis and Jessy wedding guests confirmed]]

Pesta Rakyat 1 serves 900 neighbors from RT 1-4. Pesta Rakyat 2 serves 900 neighbors from RT 5-7 and 200 employees. [[Magis and Jessy wedding guests confirmed]]

The Solo reception is expected to have about 600 guests. The Candhari Reception is for 400 Candhari neighbors, intended to address the disruption that Candhari has caused in the neighborhood. [[Magis and Jessy wedding guests confirmed]]

## Providers And Responsibilities

Candi Tirto Wedding Organizer will manage the Yogyakarta events, including Candi Tirto catering. [[Magis and Jessy wedding venues and providers confirmed]]

Mumu Wedding will provide the wedding services for the Solo reception. Deffry is confirmed as its photographer, and Jessy's neighbors are expected to prepare its catering. [[Magis and Jessy wedding venues and providers confirmed]]

Magis's mother is responsible for the family attire color scheme and is paying for the wedding events. [[Magis and Jessy wedding budget responsibility]]

## Mahar And Rings

The mahar will be a custom-made jewelry set with a necklace, rings, earrings, and a bracelet, plus 92,026 yen. [[Magis and Jessy mahar and rings confirmed]]

The couple has two kenanga-shaped engagement rings. The wedding rings for the akad will be Magis's parents' former wedding rings. [[Magis and Jessy mahar and rings confirmed]]
