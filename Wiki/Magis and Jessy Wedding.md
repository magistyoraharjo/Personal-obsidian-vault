# Magis and Jessy Wedding

Magis and Jessy's wedding is planned as a multi-day sequence spanning late September and early October. The current schedule begins with the lamaran and ends with the Candhari Reception. [[Magis and Jessy wedding schedule confirmed]]

## Current Schedule

- **26 September, morning:** lamaran.
- **26 September, evening:** Beston for Jessy's family only.
- **27 September:** siraman.
- **28 September, 10:00:** akad.
- **28 September, 19:00:** Resepsi Relasi, the first reception.
- **29 September:** off day.
- **30 September:** Pesta Rakyat 1.
- **1 October:** Pesta Rakyat 2.
- **2 October:** off day for travel to Solo and preparation for the reception there.
- **3 October:** Solo reception in Sukoharjo.
- **4 October:** off day.
- **5 October:** Candhari Reception.

## Arrangements

The Beston event is limited to Jessy's family. [[Magis and Jessy wedding schedule confirmed]]

There are no wedding rings; the plan instead includes two engagement rings. [[Perhiasan nikah]]

The March planning list identified the wedding's main cost and logistics areas: prewedding, lamaran, akad, receptions, clothing, makeup, decoration, photography, food, venues, committee, invitations, souvenirs, and entertainment. It also recorded 15 invited people from the Timboel family for each of the akad and lamaran, and 30 for the reception. [[Saturday, 28-3-2026 13꞉17꞉14 kebutuhan pernikahan]]

## Historical Context

Earlier planning proposed lamaran and Beston on 27 September, followed by akad and a single reception on 28 September. It also identified Jessy's house for the akad and left the Solo reception venue undecided. The confirmed schedule has replaced that two-day proposal with a longer sequence. [[Diskusi acara pernikahan magis jessy]]

Another earlier proposal combined the lamaran and akad on the 28th and outlined several receptions, including Solo, Tirto, and Candhari events. The current schedule confirms Resepsi Relasi, two Pesta Rakyat events, the Solo reception, and the Candhari Reception; it does not establish whether these correspond to all of the earlier proposed reception categories.
