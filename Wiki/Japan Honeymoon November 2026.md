## Things To Try By Place

Each exact dish appears once. These are targets, not required meals. Choose restaurants locally. For Magis, prioritize shellfish over finfish.

### Yokohama

- [ ] Sanma-men: noodles with stir-fried vegetables in thick sauce. Cheap meal.
- [ ] Gyu-nabe: Yokohama-style beef hotpot. Better as the higher-budget lunch.
- [ ] Napolitan: ketchup spaghetti created in Yokohama. May contain butter or cheese.

### Yaizu

- [ ] Minami-maguro or today's local-fish bowl: optional Sumaruya lunch.
- [ ] Katsuo tataki: seared bonito with a raw center.
- [ ] Grilled hamaguri or oysters: Magis's priority at Sumaruya.
- [ ] Sakura ebi kakiage: share one portion.
- [ ] Nama shirasu: raw baby sardines, only when fresh and available.

### Shizuoka City

- [ ] Shizuoka oden: include black hanpen, daikon, egg, konnyaku, and beef tendon. Cheap dinner.

### Senzu And Kawanehon

- [ ] Nagashima Dam curry: main late lunch after returning from Okuoikojo.
- [ ] Kawane tea soft serve: mainly for Jessy; contains dairy.
- [ ] Pork skewer: optional cheap snack.

### Atami

- [ ] Aji himono: grilled dried horse mackerel, useful for breakfast.
- [ ] Onsen manju: steamed bun with sweet bean filling.
- [ ] Atami pudding: dairy dessert mainly for Jessy.

### Ito And Izu

- [ ] Uzuwa-meshi: seasoned raw bullet tuna over rice, then finished with fish broth.
- [ ] Kinmedai nitsuke: simmered golden-eye snapper.
- [ ] Wasabi don: rice with fresh grated Izu wasabi. Cheap meal.

### Kawagoe

- [ ] Unaju: grilled eel over rice. Main lunch.
- [ ] Imokoi: sweet-potato and red-bean steamed cake.

### Sugamo

- [ ] Shio daifuku: lightly salted mochi with red-bean filling.

### Ueno And Ameyoko

- [ ] Menchi-katsu: fried minced-meat cutlet. Cheap snack or dinner.
- [ ] Yakitori: grilled chicken skewers. Keep order small if also eating menchi-katsu.

### Tokyo

- [ ] Edomae sushi: optional higher-budget lunch.
- [ ] Monjayaki: Tokyo-style runny savory pancake. Cheap shared dinner.
- [ ] Fukagawa-meshi: clams and rice, cooked together or served with broth. Magis's Tokyo seafood priority.

### Hong Kong

- [ ] Har gow and siu mai: shared dim sum breakfast.
- [ ] Cheung fun: steamed rice-noodle rolls.
- [ ] Century-egg and pork congee: cheap, filling breakfast dish.
- [ ] Bo lo yau: pineapple bun with butter, mainly for Jessy; contains dairy.

### Fri, 20 Nov: Yokohama -> Yaizu ([forecast](https://www.google.com/search?q=Yaizu+weather+20+November+2026))

- Eat a light, dairy-free breakfast in Yokohama. Check out around 09:00.
- Best art option: **10:00-10:45**, [Yuko Mohri, *Recompose*](https://yokohama.art.museum/eng/exhibition/202607_mohriyuko/), Yokohama Museum of Art Gallery 9. Free installation using water, everyday objects, decaying fruit, light, sound, and scent. Open until 20:00 that day, but visit at opening to protect Yaizu lunch. Skip if luggage or travel timing becomes awkward.
- Travel to Yaizu. Leave luggage at Select Inn Yaizu Ekimae before check-in if allowed; otherwise use a station locker.
- Main meal: lunch at [Sumaruya, Yaizu Fish Center](https://www.sakana-center.com/shop/4761/). It serves seafood bowls, sashimi, and grilled shellfish. Last order 16:30; cash only. [[Japan honeymoon Day 2 lunch is Sumaruya]]
- Magis: prioritize grilled hamaguri clams or grilled oysters; add a seafood or tuna bowl only if wanted.
- Jessy: Gofuku seafood bowl, finished with sea-bream broth as ochazuke.
- Find sakura ebi kakiage inside the center to share; Nagisa lists it separately for ¥1,000.
- Expected lunch total: about ¥5,000-7,000 for two.
- Return to Yaizu and check in.
- Planned early clothing stop: visit [OFF HOUSE Fujieda](https://www.hardoff.co.jp/shop/detail/?p=203163) after lunch and check-in. Fujieda is one JR stop from Yaizu. The store carries women's and men's clothing from non-brand through premium brands; current hours are 10:00-20:00. Recheck hours and local station access. [[Japan honeymoon secondhand shopping stops confirmed]]
- Do not rely on finding every needed size or layer secondhand. Buy missing essentials from a regular retailer that day rather than waiting until Seya on 27 November.
- Visit Shizuoka City only if energy remains after clothing shopping. Suruga-ya Main Store is optional.
- Optional cheap dinner: [Aoba Oden Street or Aoba Yokocho](https://www.visit-shizuoka.com/en/food/detail_463.html). Share black hanpen, daikon, egg, konnyaku, and beef tendon. Target ¥1,000-1,500 each.
