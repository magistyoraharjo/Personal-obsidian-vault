# Mas Margono

## Identity
- Production/field-work contact interviewed about earlier Kalimantan work in notes connected to Pak Timboel's commemoration. [[1000 hari bapak]] [[interview pengalaman mas margono pak desmond]]

## What I Should Remember
- Traveled to Kalimantan 6–7 times over roughly two years; trips lasted from about two to ten weeks. [[interview pengalaman mas margono pak desmond]]
- Equipment was brought from Yogyakarta, while Mbak Murti found Fuso trucks and Agus “Brengos” remained on standby in Kalimantan. [[interview pengalaman mas margono pak desmond]]
- During a serious complaint involving a detached gate-side post, he bought replacement iron in Kalimantan. [[interview pengalaman mas margono pak desmond]]
- A later welding note records a stainless MIG option using argon plus 2% CO₂, attributed to Margono in the title. [[Mungkin MIG Welding Stainless? Sudah pernah, lebih mahal (margono)]]
