# Pak Timboel

## Identity
- Also written as Pak Timbul and Pak TB in the same commemoration note. [[pak margi dalang acara 1000 hari bapak]]
- Magis's late father; the notes concern his 1,000-day commemoration and memories of his life. [[1000 hari bapak]] [[Friday, 17-4-2026 22꞉09꞉32 1000 hari bapak timboel tanggal 31 mei]]
- Described as an educator who contributed to cultural preservation. [[pak margi dalang acara 1000 hari bapak]]

## What I Should Remember
- His 1,000-day event was planned around wayang and gamelan, with “Puja Karaharjan” selected as the event title/theme and “Tirto” recorded as the final lakon. [[1000 hari bapak]] [[pak margi dalang acara 1000 hari bapak]]
- Magis and Jessy plan to use Magis's parents' former wedding rings at their akad. [[Magis and Jessy mahar and rings confirmed]]

## Relationship and Shared History
- Wangi's proposed remarks were to include memories of him, values learned from him, and hopes for the family and guests. [[1000 hari bapak]]
- Mas Margono's interview records extensive Kalimantan field work connected to memories of Pak Timboel, although individual statements are not directly attributed to Pak Timboel. [[interview pengalaman mas margono pak desmond]]
