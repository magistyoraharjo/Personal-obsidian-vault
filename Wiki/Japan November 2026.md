### Thu, 26 Nov: Ito -> Yokohama

- K's House check-out by 11:00.
- Move to Hotel LiVEMAX Yokohama-Eki Nishiguchi.
- Start secondhand shopping near Yokohama.

### Fri, 27 Nov: Yokohama Secondhand Day

Main recommendation: **BOOKOFF SUPER BAZAAR Yokohama Seya Minamidai**.

- Main store plus separate hobby building.
- Good mix: figures, model kits, anime goods, games, books, electronics, instruments, clothes, sports, tableware, household goods.
- Yokohama -> Seya by Sotetsu: about 20-22 minutes.
- Seya south exit -> store: about 15-minute walk.
- Store hours: 10:00-21:00. Recheck same day.

Easier backup: **BOOKOFF SUPER BAZAAR Olympic Higashi-Totsuka**. JR Yokosuka Line from Yokohama, then about 8-minute walk.

### Sat, 28 Nov: Yokohama -> Sugamo -> Kawagoe

- Move luggage to Sugamo Airbnb. Follow host check-in and bag instructions.
- Sugamo -> Ikebukuro by JR Yamanote.
- Ikebukuro -> Kawagoe by Tobu Tojo Line.
- Kawagoe priority: old warehouse district, Kashiya Yokocho, food exploration.

### Sun, 29 Nov: Akihabara Secondhand + Ueno

Goal: recent preowned goods. Skip new-only shops. Skip vintage-heavy Mandarake hunt.

Recommended stores:

- Suruga-ya
- AmiAmi preowned floors
- Lashinbang
- Jungle

Main goods: figures, model kits, games, mixed anime goods.

Pair with Ueno:

- Tokyo National Museum or National Museum of Nature and Science.
- Ameyoko for food.

### Mon, 30 Nov: Tokyo Flexible Day

Use for missed priorities.

- More Akihabara secondhand hunting.
- Museum recommendation: Printing Museum, Yayoi Kusama Museum, or National Museum of Modern Art.
- Food recommendations: Yanaka Ginza or Sano Miso Kameido.
- Ikebukuro option: K-BOOKS, Lashinbang, and Suruga-ya for badges, acrylics, plush, and mixed character goods.
