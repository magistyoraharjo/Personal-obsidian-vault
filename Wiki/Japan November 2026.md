## Atami fireworks

The dated itinerary's choice of 23 November is supported by the official 2026 schedule. The Atami Marine Fireworks are scheduled at Atami Bay on **Monday, 23 November 2026 from 20:20 to 20:40**, with the event planned to proceed in rain. The official site warns that Atami Station becomes extremely crowded after the display and recommends preparing return tickets or IC-card balance in advance. No paid viewing area is planned for 2026. [Atami City Tourism Association](https://www.ataminews.gr.jp/event/8/) [[japan honeymoon]]

The entries for both 22 and 23 November make an Atami stay plausible, but accommodation is not yet documented. Staying in Atami after the display would avoid depending on crowded post-fireworks travel to another city.

## Flight seating and aircraft

Aircraft assignments for November remain operational rather than guaranteed. Recent operations checked on 5 September 2026 show:

- CX780 consistently using an Airbus A330-300; [Flightradar24 history](https://www.flightradar24.com/data/flights/cx780)
- CX542 consistently using a regional Boeing 777-300; [Flightradar24 history](https://www.flightradar24.com/data/flights/cx542)
- CX501 mostly using an Airbus A330-300, with an occasional Airbus A350-900 substitution. [Flightradar24 history](https://www.flightradar24.com/data/flights/cx501)

No reliable current operating history was available for CX779. All four assignments should therefore be checked again in Cathay's Manage Booking seat maps and close to departure.

For two travellers, the best general choice on an A330 is an `A/B` or `H/K` side pair because its Economy layout is 2-4-2. On the regional 777-300, an `A/B` pair keeps the travellers together by the window but leaves the `C` seat to another passenger; `C/D` across the aisle gives both travellers aisle access. Forward regular seats are preferable for CX780 because of the Hong Kong connection. Seats next to galleys, toilets, bassinets, and the final rows should be avoided unless their particular advantages are wanted.

Exact seat numbers depend on the cabin variant and availability. Cathay identifies several apparent window seats as non-viewing on particular layouts—notably A330 seats `42A/K`, `51A/K`, or `59A/K`, and regional 777-300 seats `46A/K`, `49A/K`, and `54A/K`. The active seat map must be checked before selection. [Cathay A330 seating plans](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_ID/flying-with-us/aircraft-and-fleet/airbus-a330/a330.html) [Cathay 777 seating plans](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_ID/flying-with-us/aircraft-and-fleet/boeing-777/300.html)

Economy Light does not include free advance regular-seat selection without qualifying Cathay or oneworld status. Cathay states that preferred and regular seats become complimentary when online check-in opens—normally 24 hours before departure for travellers without qualifying status—while extra-legroom seats remain chargeable. Paying in advance for side pairs may be worthwhile if sitting together is important. [Cathay seat-selection policy](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_ID/manage-booking/travel-extras/reserve-your-seat.html)
